About the Project

Manivtha Tours & Travels

Manivtha Tours & Travels is a premium travel and transportation service provider in India. We specialize in providing highly reliable chauffeur-driven luxury sedans, family SUVs, large passenger tempo travellers, and corporate coach logistics across major cities. Known for our extreme customer focus, vehicle maintenance standards, and verified drivers, we guarantee comfort on every ride.

BRAVE Internship Program

This intelligent travel dashboard and AI Travel FAQ Chatbot was designed and deployed as a milestone project during the BRAVE Internship Program. Integrated with Google Gemini Large Language Models via custom automated n8n webhook middleware pipelines, this site provides instant answers regarding operations, payments, safety, packages, and logistics in real-time, matching standard SaaS customer support portal experiences.
